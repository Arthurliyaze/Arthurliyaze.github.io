We introduce a truck-based mobile energy courier (MEC) system to enhance grid flexibility and resilience in renewable-rich power networks. Each MEC carries onboard energy storage and power electronics to deliver energy where it’s most needed. Using deep reinforcement learning under the DDPG framework, the system optimizes routing and scheduling to minimize generation costs. Results show notable cost savings compared to traditional static storage solutions.
